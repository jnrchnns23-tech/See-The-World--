Top 10 Tourist Spot in the Philippines!

El Nido Palawan

Known for lagoons, cliffs, and island hopping.

Address: Big Lagoon, El Nido, Palawan - Serena Street, El Nido

How To Get There: Fly to El Nido, 1 hr 15 mins. Or fly to Puerto Princesa + van, 5-6 hrs.

Best Time: Nov - May Things

To Do: Tour A, Tour C, Nacpan

Beach What To Eat: Seafood, mango shake

Tips: Bring waterproof bag.

Boracay (Malay, Aklan)

Known for white sand beach and sunset.

Address: White Beach, Station 2, Balabag, Boracay, Malay, Aklan

How To Get There: Fly to Caticlan, 1 hr + boat 15 mins.

Best Time: Nov - Apr

Things To Do: Swim, sunset sailing, parasailing

What To Eat: Chori Burger, fruit shake

Tips: Book early in summer.

Siargao

Known as Surfing Capital, Cloud 9 waves

Address: Cloud 9, General Luna, Siargao, Surigao del Norte

How To Get There: Fly to Siargao, 2 hrs + van 45 mins.

Best Time: Aug - Nov for surf

Things To Do: Surfing, island hopping, Magpupungko

What To Eat: Seafood BBQ, smoothie bowl

Tips: Rent motorbike.

Cebu

Known for history, waterfalls, and lechon.

Address: Magellan's Cross, Magallanes St., Cebu City, Cebu

How To Get There: Fly to Mactan-Cebu, 1 hr 30 mins.

Best Time: Nov - May

Things To Do: Magellan's Cross, Kawasan Falls, Oslob

What To Eat: Lechon, dried mangoes

Tips: Start Oslob early.

Bohol

Known for Chocolate Hills and tarsiers.

Address: Chocolate Hills, Carmen, Bohol

How To Get There: Fly to Panglao, 1 hr 15 mins. Or Cebu + ferry 2 hrs.

Best Time: Nov - Apr

Things To Do: Chocolate Hills, Tarsier, Loboc Cruise

What To Eat: Peanut Kisses, calamay

Tips: Join countryside tour.

Banaue & Batad, Ifugao

Known for 2,000-year-old rice terraces.

Address: Banaue Viewpoint, Banaue, Ifugao - Batad, Banaue, Ifugao

How To Get There: Overnight bus from Manila, 9-10 hrs + trek 1 hr to Batad.

Best Time: Apr - May

Things To Do: Banaue Viewpoint, Batad trek, Tappiya Falls What To Eat: Pinikpikan, red rice

Tips: Wear trekking shoes.

Vigan, Ilocos Sur

Known for Spanish old town and kalesa.

Address: Calle Crisologo, Vigan City, Ilocos Sur

How To Get There: Bus from Manila, 8-9 hrs.

Best Time: Nov - Apr

Things To Do: Calle Crisologo, kalesa ride, Baluarte

What To Eat: Vigan Longganisa, empanada

Tips: Go at night for lights.

Batanes

Known for rolling hills and stone houses.

Address: Basco Lighthouse, Basco, Batanes - Marlboro Hills, Basco

How To Get There: Fly to Basco, 1 hr 30 mins.

Best Time: Feb - May

Things To Do: Basco Lighthouse, Marlboro Hills, Valugan Boulder Beach

What To Eat: Coconut crab, turmeric rice

Tips: Bring windbreaker.

Mayon Volcano (Albay, Bicol)

Known for perfect cone-shaped volcano.

Address: Cagsawa Ruins Park, Daraga, Albay

How To Get There: Fly to Legazpi, 1 hr + tricycle 20 mins.

Best Time: Nov - May

Things To Do: Cagsawa Ruins, ATV ride, Mayon Skyline

What To Eat: Bicol Express, pili nuts

Tips: Best view in the morning.

Baguio

Known for cool weather, pine trees, and strawberries.

Address: Burnham Park, Jose Abad Santos Drive, Baguio City, Benguet

How To Get There: Bus from Manila, 4-6 hours.

Best Time: Nov - Feb

Things To Do: Burnham Park, Session Road, Strawberry Farm

What To Eat: Strawberry Taho, Ube Jam

Tips: Bring jacket.


Do's & Don'ts

Do's

  • Respect local culture
  • Plan ahead
  • Keep documents secure
  • Pack important things
  • Keep the environment clean
  • Carry emergency cash
  • Stay hydrated and wear sun protection
  • Learn basic local greetings
  • Keep emergency contacts handy
  • Support local businesses

Don'ts

  • Don’t overpack
  • Don’t rely on outdated info
  • Don’t pack things you won’t need
  • Don’t arrive late
  • Don't touch or step on marine life
  • Don't ignore weather advisories
  • Don't flash expensive valuables
  • Don't feed wildlife
  • Don't forget travel insurance
  • Don't trespass on restricted areas
